## 1. What must be delivered

DALE is a substantial multi-category marketplace operated by a customer-first shopping agent. It must support real named products, evidence-linked comparisons, device/accessory matching, optional exact-product group savings, separately approved PayPal checkout, private orders and customer-selected remedies. Catalog expansion is required to make these workflows useful; storefront volume alone does not qualify the AI product.

The current 142 offers / 138 previously counted families are an incomplete starter assortment. ## 2. Binding counting rules

1. The unit of coverage is a **leaf product type × canonical brand × distinct manufacturer model**, not a whole department. Headphones, earbuds, speakers and soundbars have separate targets. Portable SSDs, internal SSDs, HDDs, memory cards and USB flash drives have separate targets. Clothing types are separately counted.
2. Default minimum: **10 eligible brands per leaf type and 10 distinct models per eligible brand**. Standard depth target: 10–15 models per brand. This normally produces 100–150 models per type. Adding up to 15 eligible brands can expand a type to 150–225; 15 × 15 is 225, not 150.
3. The user's availability exception applies per brand/type: if fewer than ten real qualifying models exist, include all qualifying models and record a manufacturer-backed supply exception. No guessed shortage, silent omission, fake model, storage/color/size inflation or unrelated product substitution is allowed. An exception records the enumerated models, category, source region/date, excluded items/reasons and actual eligible count.
4. Aim for 100–150 distinct models in every leaf type. If legitimate supply exceptions reduce that total, add other relevant brands where sourcing supports them. If the whole type still cannot meet its target, it remains **supply-limited with an explicit shortfall**; it is not reported as an ordinary completed 100-model category.
5. Use current documented product ranges first. A named prior generation can be included when still offered and clearly labelled; an archived/discontinued comparison record is separate and cannot masquerade as current stock. A source checked in 2026 is not automatically a 2026 model.
6. A separately documented generation/model can count; RAM, storage capacity, color, clothing/shoe size, bottle volume and cosmetic strap/lens choices are variants. Model/reference identity must be reviewed before counting. Audit the existing 138-family baseline under these rules; do not preserve an inflated family total for appearance.
7. Consumer brand, parent company and product line are separate fields. Alienware remains discoverable as a Dell line; repeated Jaeger/LeCoultre spellings resolve to Jaeger-LeCoultre. Duplicate brand aliases and cross-listed SKUs cannot increase coverage.
8. Report distinct models, variants and merchant offers separately. A canonical product appearing under phone accessories and laptop accessories retains one inventory identity.

The earlier examples of 60–70 phones/laptops and 30–40 tablets are smaller than the user's final general requirement. This revision uses the stronger final rule: 10 eligible brands × 10 models where real supply supports it, with the documented exception above. It does not cap tablets at forty or phones at seventy.

## 5. Size budget and what completion means

The category matrix above contains 21 electronics leaf types, 24 clothing/lifestyle leaf types and five home/appliance leaf types: **50 type targets**. At the nominal 100-model floor this is **5,000 model placements**; at 150 per type it is **7,500 placements**. The former global 2,000-family target is too small for this matrix and is superseded as a completion criterion.

Placements are a sourcing budget, not a promise of 5,000 currently verified unique models. Cross-listed products are deduplicated globally, real supply exceptions reduce achievable counts, and exact totals are finalized after manufacturer censuses. Publish both unique catalog totals and the complete 50-row coverage ledger. A large global total cannot conceal a one-laptop category.

For each brand/type, the ledger stores target, enumerated eligible supply, reviewed models, staged models, published models, decoded-media coverage, agent-qualified models, source freshness and exception status. State values: not enumerated, collecting, review required, staged, published, qualified, or documented supply-limited. An unverified shortage stays open.

Release-level completion requires every scheduled type to meet its count/media/data/agent/commerce/operations gates, or carry the user's allowed **source-proven** supply exception. Planning, code completion, catalog coverage and live-provider qualification are reported separately. Never turn passing tests for a 142-record build into a claim of completed assortment.

## 6. Sourcing and ingestion at the requested scale

1. Census every brand/type before coding another small showcase batch: exact current models, manufacturer URLs, canonical references, region, generation/lifecycle and exclusion reasons. Freeze a coverage manifest with the required count and any factual supply exception.
2. Use manufacturer/authorized feeds and permitted public sources. India is the preferred commercial region; imported global facts disclose their region. Do not scrape Amazon seller prices/images into invented DALE stock. Official product facts and real merchant offers have different provenance.
3. Implement reusable **category-specific** extractors and normalization. Store observations and evidence per field, not a marketing description treated as a verified specification. Missing facts remain unknown and contradictions require review.
4. Build a durable collection/refresh queue: idempotent job keys, per-origin rate/concurrency limits, bounded retries/backoff, resumable records, failed-job review and retained observations. Existing local checkpoint tooling is reused, not mistaken for the complete hosted job queue.
5. Validate exact-model media, source rights/attribution, image size/type and photographed variant. Use bounded image delivery/storage; do not attempt to cache thousands of originals on the current nearly full EC2 root volume.
6. Stage imports in bounded batches; validate the **whole scheduled category census** before declaring it populated. Atomically publish reviewed records, retain versioned source revisions, permit withdrawal/restoration and keep historical order identities.
7. Refresh specs, lifecycle and offers under separate policies. Missing merchant feeds keep explicitly sandbox prices/stock. Stale financial terms require reconfirmation; a manufacturer list price is not a PayPal-ready seller offer.

Publication remains privileged. External product text/images cannot grant agent permissions, replace a SKU identity or authorize a payment.

## 7. Category contracts and accessory graph

Before publishing each leaf type, add its typed filter/comparison contract and test it against reviewed manufacturer examples. Electronics need exact interfaces/protocols and units; clothing needs manufacturer references, material/size charts; fragrance needs concentration/volume and attributed notes; watches need movement/dimensions; sunglasses need frame/lens dimensions and explicit lens properties; skincare needs formulation/ingredient version evidence.

Represent compatibility as **fits / does not fit / unknown**, backed by exact device/accessory observations and prerequisites. A charger can serve multiple phones/laptops without duplicating its SKU. Record output profiles, required cable, port sharing and proprietary speed restrictions. Do not create one fictional charger per phone or treat USB-C as sufficient proof. Include affordable verified Ambrane/Portronics options alongside original and other third-party accessories. Compare total required accessory cost, not just the adapter price.

The agent retrieves bounded candidates from the database, checks hard constraints before ranking, cites facts and asks material clarifications. “Find a charger for my MacBook and reduce cost through a group” must use the specific device and available group terms, without a preset scenario sequence. Cross-category bundles retain separate SKU constraints and combined budgets. A missing known fact causes an uncertainty/clarification, not a confident fabricated answer.

The original seven capabilities remain: AgentGuard, BuyTogether, ScamPause, Buyer's Advocate, ReturnShield, PartsMatch and RescueMyOrder. Returns/refunds/replacements retain customer priority, party-owned evidence, uncertainty, appeal/human review and separately authorized financial actions.

## 9. Acceptance and engineer demonstration

- **Coverage:** verify every brand/type has at least ten distinct models or the full source-enumerated smaller range; verify ten eligible brands per type or an explicit unresolved supply shortfall. Reject duplicate/variant/cross-list inflation.
- **Data/media:** exact reference and key fact source associations, contradictory/missing facts, current/prior/discontinued labels, source age, correctly decoded photographs, visible photo failure and unsafe-image rejection.
- **Search:** per-type brand/model filters, full pagination without duplicates/omissions under specified revision semantics, stable candidate eligibility/ranking and exact variants beyond the cheapest hundred.
- **AI:** held-out natural language/paraphrases for each leaf type, unknown models, contradictory requests, multi-category budgets, compatible cheaper accessories, factual comparison and unavailable groups. Fixture tests establish orchestration contracts; live inference is separately qualified.
- **Commerce:** two independent browser profiles/laptops create accounts or guests and commit to the same exact SKU group. Database counts independent customers, updates shared readiness, preserves guest conversion and requires each shopper's quote approval. Test duplicate/retried/concurrent commitments, stock shortages, stale terms and no cross-user order exposure.
- **After purchase:** owned order help, customer/seller evidence, wrong-item ambiguity, customer-selected refund/replacement, reviewer/appeal paths and no unapproved financial action. Signed provider receipts and genuine sandbox capture/refund remain separate from simulated tests.
- **Operations:** production PostgreSQL, 10,000-record isolated query tests, staged representative 5,000+ model workload, 50/100 concurrent catalog reads and 10/25 concurrent customer workflows with declared p95/error targets and actual CPU/RAM/disk measurements. Do not load thousands of synthetic offers into the public store. Test queue retries/recovery, migrations, rollback/backup restore, service restart and private-data boundaries.

The 50/100 and 10/25 concurrency levels are workload targets, not present capacity claims. The current read-only 50-request burst at concurrency ten against 142 offers is a baseline only. Measure actual host capacity; do not assume the earlier owner-reported 4 GiB when the latest machine read reports about 1.9 GiB. Any paid capacity/storage change is a separate owner decision after evidence.

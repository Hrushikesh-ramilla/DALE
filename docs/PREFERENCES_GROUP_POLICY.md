# Shopper preferences and merchant group policy

Checkpoint 3 implementation, 8 October 2026. Production and hosted release evidence must be recorded by the release owner before closing the checkpoint.

The shopping brief accepts explicit price and feature weights between 0 and 100, with a positive combined weight. The interface uses a slider totaling 100. Eligibility always filters device fit, category and maximum budget first. Price scoring is the fraction of the customer's budget remaining; feature scoring is whether the shopper's named preference appears in the product's recorded specifications. The weighted score is normalized by the combined weights. Ties use price and product ID. Sponsorship never participates. Missing feature evidence receives no feature match. Without explicit weights, the existing price/features ordering remains available. This is bounded, auditable ranking rather than invented product quality or delivery scoring.

The reviewer persona represents the demo merchant's authorized policy approver. In **Environment details → Merchant group pricing**, set the default required shoppers, discount percent, formation deadline and checkout window. A product-specific tier can override the default shopper threshold and discount; it inherits the configured time windows. The policy applies within the private storefront workspace, keeping independent engineering scenarios isolated. Buyers and sellers cannot approve a policy. Every approval increments a policy version and records its reviewer in the audit. Saving an obsolete version fails, including concurrent approvals.

At formation, each group records its policy version, base price, discounted price, threshold and checkout window. Future policy edits apply to newly formed groups. An existing group does not receive a changed discount or threshold. No existing quote, successful purchase or financial operation is repriced. A conversational commitment checks the displayed policy version again, including under the workspace transaction lock. A changed policy requires refreshed offers. The server checks stock before finalizing; unavailable thresholds cannot create new commitments. Defaults preserve two shoppers, 10% off and a 30-minute checkout window.

## Manual acceptance

1. In a private fresh demo, select the 65W USB-C laptop profile and chargers, a USD 80 budget and feature `100W`. Enable explicit weights and move price weight to zero. Save with **Find my match**. The 100W charger should rank first. Reload: price weight remains zero and feature weight remains 100. Move price weight to 100 and save: the USD 29 65W charger ranks first. A USD 30 ceiling still excludes the USD 49 100W charger at any weight.
2. Switch the owned demo to Reviewer. Open **Environment details → Merchant group pricing**. Set three shoppers, 20% off and a 45-minute checkout window, then approve. Switch back to Shopper and form a group for the USD 29 charger. It shows one of three commitments, a conditional USD 23.20 price and no payment.
3. Change the policy after that group forms. Join the existing group as the other shoppers. Its original three-shopper/20% terms remain. A new group for another product uses the new settings. Successful checkout retains its locked price after further edits.
4. Add a product-specific tier using that product's selector and the current member/discount values. On approval, that product uses its tier; unrelated products use the defaults. Duplicate or unknown products, invalid ranges and product thresholds exceeding stock are rejected.
5. Submit a policy update as Buyer or Seller: it fails without changing settings. Two approvals using the same version produce one successful update and one stale-version error. Old conversational offers cannot commit to newly changed policy terms; refresh them.

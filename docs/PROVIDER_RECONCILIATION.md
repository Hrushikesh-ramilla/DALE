# External provider adjustment reconciliation

Verified PayPal webhook notifications are wake-up signals. The durable receipt and associated per-order observations are saved before acknowledgement, including for completed orders. Unverified notifications cannot alter observations or finances. Worker reads use the sandbox dispute, refund and capture GET endpoints; no notification initiates a capture, refund or replacement.

Each observation records its provider reference, exact local capture, processing state, retry schedule and explanation. Pending, mismatched, active or ambiguous observations pause further automatic refund/replacement work while keeping the customer claim and review route open. Failed reads retain durable bounded backoff. Out-of-order dispute facts cannot regress a recorded provider timestamp. Existing legacy records need no destructive migration.

A completed refund is accounted only after matching its unique refund ID, exact capture link, USD amount and captured balance. An already recorded internal refund reference is not credited twice. External partial refunds reduce the remaining balance. A pending external refund cannot authorize another remedy; a capture reversal status alone does not establish a refund amount or clear review.

PayPal's dispute `amount_refunded` can describe money paid by either the merchant or PayPal. It is disclosed separately as a provider-reported customer payout; it is **never added to merchant refunds without a verified refund reference**. Buyer-favour, payout and unknown outcomes require review to avoid duplicate reimbursement. A matched seller-favour or buyer-canceled terminal dispute with no reported payout can clear the external block while preserving the merchant's customer policy. [Official Disputes API specification](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/paypal/paypal-rest-api-specifications/main/openapi/customer_disputes_v1.json)

Supported wake-ups are `CUSTOMER.DISPUTE.CREATED`, `.UPDATED`, `.RESOLVED`, `PAYMENT.CAPTURE.REFUNDED` and `.REVERSED`. For an unlinked refund/capture adjustment, capture status is retained for human reconciliation rather than inventing a refund. [Official webhook events](https://developer.paypal.com/api/rest/webhooks/event-names/)

Before a new refund/replacement, the server reads current capture facts and lists disputes by the exact capture. Worker discovery is limited to two eligible orders per workspace per tick and one scan per order every ten minutes. A next-page link or missing list fields cannot establish absence of disputes and therefore leaves remedies paused for review. A missed-webhook refunded/partially refunded capture cannot invent an amount or authorize another remedy without verified references. Genuine financial acceptance remains separate. Fixture workspaces never call provider reads even if global mode later changes.

An authorized reviewer can open **PayPal reconciliation** on a sandbox order and select **Refresh provider facts**. They can optionally supply up to five exact known refund references; these are GET lookup hints, not financial authority. Provider capture/currency/amount verification must succeed before a reference is credited once. Exact completed refund references for the full capture can clear a refunded-capture review. For a partially refunded capture, unique verified references must account for the entire recorded refunded balance before review can clear; an explicit approved refund can then request only the remaining balance. A completed reference cannot change its credited amount on refresh, and an earlier unknown refund operation that exceeds the new remaining balance cannot be reissued. Active disputes, ambiguous payouts or unavailable facts remain visible for human/provider follow-up; a note or model cannot override them. The case export includes these observations separately from merchant refunds.

Development validation: provider-adjustment, typed-adapter, recovery and refund contracts cover signature rejection, completed-order wake-ups, duplicate/out-of-order records, pending and ambiguous payouts, partial refunds, internal/external reference duplication, invalid currency/capture/amount, durable retry, missed-webhook discovery, incomplete pagination, preflight outages and read-only reviewer reconciliation. Release validation records final counts and production checks separately; no live provider request or spend was needed for these contracts.

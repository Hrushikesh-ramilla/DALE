Take a look around the WH-1000XM4 headphones
The technology that makes it happen

Dual noise sensor technology, featuring two microphones on each earcup, captures ambient noise and passes the data to the HD Noise Cancelling Processor QN1. Using a new algorithm, the HD Noise Cancelling Processor QN1 then applies noise-cancelling processing in real time to a range of acoustic environments. Working together with a new Bluetooth® Audio SoC (System on Chip), it senses and adjusts to music and noise signals, as well as acoustic characteristics between the driver unit and ears, at over 700 times per second.

Sound you can believe in

A built-in analogue amplifier integrated in the HD Noise Cancelling Processor QN1 realises unmatched signal-to-noise ratio for low distortion and exceptional sound quality for portable devices. Powerful 40-mm drivers, with Liquid Crystal Polymer (LCP) diaphragms, make the headphones perfect for handling heavy beats and can reproduce a full range of frequencies up to 40 kHz.

The WH-1000XM4 supports High-Resolution Audio and High-Resolution Audio Wireless, thanks to LDAC™, our industry-adopted audio coding technology. LDAC transmits approximately three times more data3 than conventional Bluetooth® audio for exceptional High-Resolution Audio quality.4

Learn more about LDAC
Real-time restoration of all your compressed files

Using Edge-AI (Artificial Intelligence), DSEE Extreme™ (Digital Sound Enhancement Engine) upscales compressed digital music files in real time. Dynamically recognising instrumentation, musical genres and individual elements of each song, such as vocals or interludes, it restores the high-range sound lost in compression for a richer, more complete listening experience.

Specifications and Features

Intelligent and intuitive, the WH-1000XM4 headphones offer further improvements to our advanced noise cancellation, exceptional sound quality, and smart listening that adjusts to your behaviour and your location.

  • HD Noise-Cancelling Processor QN1, Bluetooth Audio SoC and a dual noise sensor let you listen without distractions

  • Personal noise-cancelling optimiser and atmospheric pressure optimising

  • High-quality audio with DSEE Extreme™ and LDAC

  • Adaptive Sound Control automatically adjusts ambient sound settings to suit your location and behaviour

  • Wireless freedom with BLUETOOTH® technology
